Lab: `ai2-olmo` (Allen Institute for AI, nonprofit). Projects: `olmo2`, `olmo3`, `olmo-ladder`, `olmo-hybrid`.
Environment caveat: arxiv.org, allenai.org, huggingface.co and most mirrors were blocked by the egress proxy in this session. Paper numbers below come from search-engine summaries of the papers (URLs given) plus files read directly from Ai2's GitHub repos (`allenai/OLMo`, `allenai/OLMo-core`), which were reachable. Every number that could not be traced to a URL is listed under "Verification status".

## 1. The experiment process

**Model ladder (2024).** Bhagia et al., "Establishing Task Scaling Laws via Compute-Efficient Model Ladders" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04403; code/checkpoints https://github.com/allenai/OLMo-ladder) trained 16 "ladder" models: 4 sizes (190M, 370M, 760M, 1.3B non-embedding params) x 4 Chinchilla multiples (1xC, 2xC, 5xC, 10xC, i.e. 20N to 200N tokens). The ladder cost ~1% of the compute of the two targets it predicted, OLMo 2 7B-4T and 13B-5T. The law is two-step: (N,D) -> per-task loss via L = A/N^alpha + B/D^beta + E, then a sigmoid from task loss to accuracy. Reported accuracy: within 2 absolute points for MMLU, HellaSwag, PIQA, SocialIQA on both targets; average 6.9 points on the four noisier tasks; ~4 points averaged over eight. The fitted A, B, alpha, beta, E per task are in the paper's tables but were not retrievable here. Ladder tooling now lives in OLMo-core (`model_ladder` module, https://olmo-core.readthedocs.io/en/latest/model_ladder.html).

**OLMo 2 (Nov 2024 - Apr 2025).** "2 OLMo 2 Furious" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00656; 40 authors). Development pattern: (a) architecture/stability changes (RMSNorm reordered norm, QK-norm, z-loss) ablated at 1B against the OLMo-0724 baseline, with 7B loss-spike comparisons; the paper notes the 1B findings transferred to 7B/13B/32B. (b) Data: 19 "microanneal" runs totaling 130B tokens (less than three 50B anneals) to test individual Dolmino sources. (c) Final recipe: stage 1 on OLMo-mix-1124 (7B: ~3.9T = 1 epoch; 13B: 5T = 1.2 epochs; 32B: 6.056T, scheduled 6.5T; 1B: 4T), then stage-2 anneals in several seeds that are weight-averaged ("souped": 7B 3x50B; 13B 3x100B + 1x300B; 32B 100B + 100B + 300B; https://github.com/allenai/OLMo-core/blob/main/src/scripts/official/OLMo2/README.md). Hardware: Jupiter (1024 H100, InfiniBand) and Augusta (Google Cloud A3 Mega, 160 nodes x 8 H100 for the 32B, ~1800 tok/s/GPU, ~38% MFU per https://allenai.org/blog/olmo2-32b). Energy (paper Table 19): 7B 131 MWh / 52 tCO2eq; 13B 257 MWh / 101 tCO2eq. GPU-hours for 7B/13B were not published. Logs: W&B for 7B/13B, comet.ml for 32B (links in the OLMo-core README).

**Morrison et al. 2025 (ICLR), "Holistically Evaluating the Environmental Impact of Creating Language Models"** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05804) is the OLMo 2-era accounting: models from 20M to 13B trained on up to 5.6T tokens; 493 tCO2e and 2.769M L water in total; development (hyperparameter tuning and ablations before the main runs) ~50% of the training footprint.

**OLMo 3 (Nov 2025).** Tech report https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13961 (66 authors). Data work uses a two-part framework: distributed per-source "microanneals" (5B target tokens + 5B web, compared with a 10B web-only baseline) feeding centralized "integration tests" (full 100B midtraining anneals on candidate mixes, judged on base quality and post-trainability). Final recipe (https://github.com/allenai/OLMo-core/blob/main/src/scripts/official/OLMo3/README.md): 7B stage 1 5.93T tokens on 512 H100 (the report/blog say "up to 1,024"), 100B midtrain on 128 H100, 50B long-context on 256 H100; 32B stage 1 5.50T on 1024 H100, two 100B midtraining ingredients on 512 H100 souped, 100B long-context on 1024 H100 with the last three checkpoints souped. The official scripts expose run-management decisions that are usually invisible: the 7B schedule was extended from 5T to 7T mid-run (`OLMo-3-1025-7B-pretrain-2.py`, HalfCosWithWarmup continuation) and then hard-stopped at one epoch; the 32B midtraining branched from step 656000 (5.50T) while the pretraining run continued to step 679000 (5.70T), so ~196B tokens of 32B pretraining were not used by the released model. Internal run names (`OLMo25`, `stego32-highlr-filter3`) hint at prior variants. W&B reports for both runs are public (wandb.ai/ai2-llm/Olmo-3-1025-7B, Olmo-3-1125-32B). Throughput/energy (tech report as summarized at https://muxup.com/2025q4/minipost-olmo3-training-cost): 7B ~234k H100-h, 7.7k tok/s/GPU, 146 MWh GPU energy, 621 W/GPU; 32B ~1.05M H100-h, ~1.9k tok/s/GPU, 681 MWh GPU energy, 649 W/GPU, all inclusive of restarts/evals/checkpointing.

**Olmo 3.1 (Dec 16, 2025).** RL for 32B Think resumed for 21 more days on 224 GPUs (https://huggingface.co/allenai/Olmo-3.1-32B-Think), +5 AIME, +4 ZebraLogic, +4 IFEval, +20 IFBench.

**Olmo Hybrid (Mar-Apr 2026).** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03444, blog https://allenai.org/blog/olmohybrid, scripts https://github.com/allenai/OLMo-core/blob/main/src/scripts/official/OLMo-hybrid/README.md. Progression: 1B-scale sweep (hybrid GDN > pure GDN > transformer > hybrid Mamba2 > pure Mamba2 on BPB) -> scaling ladder of 7 sizes x 5 Chinchilla multiples (D/N in {10,20,40,80,160}) for three architectures -> 7B run matched in params/throughput to Olmo 3 7B (d_model 3840, 30 heads, 3:1 GDN:attention), 5.93T stage-1 tokens, 2x100B midtrain souped, 100B long-context, on 512 GPUs (H100, then Lambda B200 from ~halfway; B200 phase 3T tokens in 6.19 days, https://lambda.ai/blog/open-model-open-metrics-how-lambda-and-the-olmo-team-trained-olmo-hybrid). Chinchilla fit: exponents indistinguishable across architectures (fixed-exponent fit alpha=beta=0.22); data coefficient B hybrid 83.7 [80.2, 87.1] vs transformer 94.9 [88.7, 102.0]; A 70.1 vs 71.8; E 1.569 vs 1.597 in the fixed-exponent fit; projected 1.3-1.9x token savings from 1B to 70B; compute-optimal projections over 1e18-1e23 FLOPs.

## 2. What Morrison et al. (2026) found

"The Hidden Cost of Thinking: Energy Use and Environmental Impact of LMs Beyond Pretraining" (Jacob Morrison et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01158, May 2026) accounts for the entire Olmo 3 pipeline (7B and 32B, Instruct and Think): 8.34M GPU-hours total, of which development (experimentation, failed runs, ablations) is 6.85M = 82.2% and final runs 1.49M. The authors describe this as ~65% higher than the ~50% development share found for the pretraining-focused OLMo 2 pipeline (their 2025 paper). Totals: ~12.3 GWh datacenter energy, 4,251 tCO2eq, 15,887 kL water. Final-run detail: 32B pretraining 1.05M GPU-h and 1,423 MWh DC energy; 7B pretraining 234k GPU-h; Think-specific post-training (DPO + RLVR) ~10K GPU-h (7B) and ~97K (32B) vs Instruct-specific ~4.2K and ~7.1K; 32B Think post-training 98,464 DC kWh vs 5,659 for Instruct (17x), driven by RLVR rollout generation (generator = 87% of Think post-training energy, ~600 W/GPU vs 130-220 W for the trainer; RLVR trainer tokens 18.7B vs 564M). A press summary: https://charonhub.deeplearning.ai/a-full-accounting-of-models-gpu-use/ ("greatest impacts came from experimentation and synthetic data generation"). Implication for this report: experimental:final compute ratio ~4.6:1 by GPU-hours for Olmo 3, versus ~1:1 for OLMo 2.

## 3. What is reconstructable

- Final-run token counts to the step: checkpoint CSVs in OLMo-core list every stage checkpoint with tokens seen (7B: 5,929,965,715,456 stage-1 tokens; 32B: 5,695,864,832,000 at end of stage 1; Hybrid 6,231,074,799,616 final). Intermediate checkpoints are downloadable (olmo-checkpoints.org), so loss/eval curves can be recomputed.
- Loss curves: public W&B (Olmo 3, Hybrid), comet.ml (OLMo 2 32B) dashboards; no loss values were transcribed here.
- GPU-hours: reported for Olmo 3 pretraining and post-training (Morrison 2026); derived here for OLMo 2 32B (tokens / throughput) and Olmo 3 midtraining (assumes pretraining throughput). Not available for OLMo 2 7B/13B/1B.
- Ablation counts: OLMo 2 microanneals (19 runs, 130B tokens) are explicit; Olmo 3 microanneal/integration-test counts and the per-stage split of the 6.85M development GPU-hours were not retrieved.
- Dollar costs: none published; cost_usd_est uses USD 2/H100-hour and is only indicative (Ai2's compute is largely donated/partner-provided: Google Cloud, Lambda, NSF/NVIDIA USD 152M OMAI award of Aug 2025, https://allenai.org/blog/nsf-nvidia, whose Blackwell cluster came online May 2026 and post-dates Olmo 3).
